      Contrast checks

      Text needs 4.5:1 (WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.3). Buttons and other interactive parts need 3:1 to stand out (SC 1.4.11). 4.49:1 is a fail.

      CheckRatioNeedsResult

      Buttons & focus

      Every button needs a hover, pressed and disabled look, and keyboard users need a visible focus ring (3:1 against the background, WCAG 2.2 SC 2.4.13).

      CheckRatioNeedsResult

      Dark mode

      Dark mode remaps the roles rather than inverting colours. Surfaces get lighter as they rise, and brand colours get lighter and slightly calmer so they don't glow.

      Dark mode contrast checks

      Colour blindness check

      How the main and status colours look to people with each type of colour blindness. Where two colours merge, add a second cue such as an icon, label or underline.

      Colour-blind-safe chart colours (Okabe-Ito)

      For charts, maps and graphs where colour carries the data. These stay distinct for most kinds of colour blindness.

      Shades

      Lighter and darker versions on an even lightness scale. Hover a shade to see what it's for; the outlined one is closest to the colour itself.

      Status colours
